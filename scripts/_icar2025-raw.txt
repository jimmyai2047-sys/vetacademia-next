Q.No. 01. Non-standard genetic code is known for one of the following:

(A) Nuclear genome only

(B) Mitochondrial genome only

(C) Both nuclear and mitochondrial genomes

(D) Genetic code is universal

Correct Answer: (C) Both nuclear and mitochondrial genomes

Explanation: Non-standard (deviant) genetic codes exist in both nuclear genomes of some organisms (e.g., ciliates, some fungi/yeasts) and in mitochondrial genomes across many species, so the standard/universal code has exceptions in both compartments.



Q.No. 02. The phenomenon in which the template strand and its copy shift their relative position to change the copy number of the template is called as

(A) Replication fork

(B) Frameshift mutation

(C) Replication slippage

(D) Synonymous mutation

Correct Answer: (C) Replication slippage

Explanation: Replication slippage is the phenomenon where the template and nascent strand misalign at repetitive sequences, causing insertion or deletion of repeat units and altering copy number.



Q.No. 03. The length of Okazaki fragments in Eukaryotes is one of the following:

(A) 100-200 nt

(B) 300-400 nt

(C) 500-800 nt

(D) 1000-2000 nt

Correct Answer: (A) 100-200 nt

Explanation: In eukaryotes, Okazaki fragments on the lagging strand are shorter than in prokaryotes, typically about 100-200 nucleotides, corresponding to the nucleosomal spacing.
